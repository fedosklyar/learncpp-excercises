Here we had the material on scientific notation and the task of converting some numbers
according to the rules of this notation. we need to specify the significant part of the number
along with the power of 10 using ('e') 

1) 34.50 = 3.450e1              with 4 significant
2) 0.004000 = 4.000e-3          with 4 significant
3) 123.005 = 1.23005e2          with 6 significant
4) 146000 = 1.46e5              with 3 significant
5) 146000.001 = 1.46000001e5    with 9 significant
6) 0.0000000008 = 8e-10         with 1 significant
7) 34500.0 = 3.45000e4          with 6 significant
8) 146000 = 1.46000e5           with 6 significant
